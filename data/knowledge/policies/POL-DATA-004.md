
# Data Classification and Handling Policy

Document ID: POL-DATA-004
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Owner: Security Operations
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose
Protect organizational information according to its sensitivity.

## 2. Scope
Applies to employees, contractors, and authorized IT personnel.

## 3. Classification Levels
- employee_public: General internal information available to all employees.
- it_internal: Operational information restricted to authorized IT personnel.
- admin_restricted: Sensitive administrative information restricted to IT Administrators.

## 4. Handling Requirements
- Access must follow the principle of least privilege.
- Information must be stored in approved company systems.
- Sensitive information must not be shared through unauthorized channels.
- Access permissions must be reviewed when employee roles change.

## 5. Prohibited Actions
- Sharing restricted information with unauthorized users.
- Uploading confidential documents to unapproved services.
- Bypassing access-control restrictions.

## 6. Incident Reporting
Suspected data exposure must be reported to Security Operations.
