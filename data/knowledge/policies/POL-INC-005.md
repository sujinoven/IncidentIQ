
# Incident Response and Escalation Policy

Document ID: POL-INC-005
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Owner: Security Operations
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose
Ensure consistent identification, investigation, and escalation of IT incidents.

## 2. Scope
Applies to IT Support, service owners, and incident responders.

## 3. Severity Classification
- P1: Critical business service unavailable or widespread disruption.
- P2: Major degradation affecting multiple users.
- P3: Limited disruption with an available workaround.
- P4: Minor issue with low business impact.

## 4. Incident Handling
- Record the affected service and observed symptoms.
- Assign a severity based on business impact.
- Check monitoring alerts and relevant historical incidents.
- Follow approved troubleshooting runbooks.
- Document evidence, actions, and outcomes.

## 5. Escalation
- P1 incidents require immediate escalation to the incident response lead.
- P2 incidents require escalation to the responsible service team.
- Security incidents must be escalated to Security Operations.

## 6. Restrictions
Unauthorized personnel must not modify production infrastructure.
Incident responders must not bypass security controls to restore service.
