
# Backup and Recovery Policy

Document ID: POL-BKP-007
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Owner: Infrastructure Operations
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose
Protect critical business data and support recovery after service failures.

## 2. Scope
Applies to company-managed production systems and approved data repositories.

## 3. Backup Requirements
- Critical systems must follow documented backup schedules.
- Backup jobs must be monitored for failures.
- Backup data must be protected against unauthorized access.
- Recovery procedures must be tested periodically.
- Backup failures must be recorded and investigated.

## 4. Recovery Requirements
- Recovery actions require authorization from the responsible service owner.
- Recovery points must be verified before restoration.
- Restoration activities must be documented.
- Service functionality must be validated after recovery.

## 5. Prohibited Actions
- Restoring production data without authorization.
- Disclosing backup credentials.
- Deleting backup records without approved procedures.

## 6. Escalation
Failed recovery attempts must be escalated to Infrastructure Operations.
