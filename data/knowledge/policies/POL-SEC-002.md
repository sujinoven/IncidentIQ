
# Password and MFA Security Policy

Document ID: POL-SEC-002
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Owner: Identity Operations
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose

Protect corporate accounts against unauthorized access.

## 2. Scope

Applies to employees, contractors, and administrators
using NovaTech corporate systems.

## 3. Password Requirements

- Passwords must not be shared.
- Users must not disclose passwords to IT Support.
- Password resets must use approved recovery procedures.
- Suspected compromised credentials must be reported.

## 4. MFA Requirements

- MFA is mandatory for supported corporate systems.
- Users must not share MFA approval codes.
- Unexpected MFA prompts must be reported.
- MFA bypass is prohibited without an approved,
  documented exception process.

## 5. Account Recovery

Users who lose access must contact IT Support.

IT Support must verify the user's identity before
initiating the approved password reset process.

## 6. Escalation

Suspected account compromise must be escalated
to Identity Operations.
