
# Acceptable Technology Use Policy

Document ID: POL-USE-008
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Owner: Security Operations
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose
Define responsible use of NovaTech technology resources.

## 2. Scope
Applies to employees, contractors, and authorized users.

## 3. Acceptable Use
- Company systems must be used for authorized business activities.
- Users must protect company accounts and devices.
- Security incidents must be reported promptly.
- Software installations must follow company approval processes.

## 4. Prohibited Actions
- Sharing corporate credentials.
- Attempting unauthorized access to systems or data.
- Installing malicious or unapproved software.
- Disabling company security controls.
- Transferring restricted information to unauthorized destinations.

## 5. Reporting
Suspected misuse or security violations must be reported to IT Support or Security Operations.

## 6. Escalation
Security Operations reviews suspected policy violations.
