# Cannot Sign In to Corporate SSO

Document ID: KB-ID-003
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains cannot sign in to corporate sso and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- SSO login fails
- Authentication error appears

## Possible Causes
- Account access problem
- Identity service disruption

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check the service status
2. Use approved account recovery
3. Contact IT Support if access remains blocked

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
