# Understanding Service Health Alerts

Document ID: KB-MON-014
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains understanding service health alerts and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- Monitoring dashboard shows degraded service

## Possible Causes
- Health-check failure
- Service dependency issue

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Review alert timestamps
2. Check affected services
3. Correlate with active incidents

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
