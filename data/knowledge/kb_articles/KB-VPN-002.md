# VPN Disconnects Frequently

Document ID: KB-VPN-002
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains vpn disconnects frequently and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- VPN disconnects during work
- Frequent reconnection prompts

## Possible Causes
- Unstable internet connection
- VPN service degradation

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check internet stability
2. Reconnect using the approved VPN client
3. Report repeated disconnections to IT Support

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
