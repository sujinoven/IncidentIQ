# Email Not Received

Document ID: KB-EMAIL-005
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains email not received and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- Expected email has not arrived

## Possible Causes
- Delivery delay
- Incorrect recipient address

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check spam and quarantine notifications
2. Confirm sender details
3. Report persistent delivery issues

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
