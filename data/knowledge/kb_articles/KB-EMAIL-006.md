# Email Attachment Rejected

Document ID: KB-EMAIL-006
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains email attachment rejected and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- Attachment is blocked or rejected

## Possible Causes
- File type restriction
- Security scanning policy

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check the rejection notice
2. Use approved file-sharing methods
3. Contact IT Support for assistance

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
