# Payroll Portal Unavailable

Document ID: KB-PAY-007
Organization: NovaTech Solutions
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-01

## Overview
This article explains payroll portal unavailable and provides
approved initial troubleshooting guidance.

## Common Symptoms
- Payroll portal does not load

## Possible Causes
- Application outage
- Authentication failure

## Safe Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check published service status
2. Retry later if degraded
3. Report the issue without sharing payroll data

## Safety Guidance
- Never share passwords or MFA codes.
- Never disable company security controls.
- Do not perform unauthorized system changes.

## Escalation
Contact the responsible IT service team if the
problem persists or affects multiple users.
